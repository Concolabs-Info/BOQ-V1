Roof implementation checks
--------------------------
- Roof source candidates come from Pre-classified/included roof evidence only.
- Confirmed Pre scale is required for measured roof geometry.
- Source pixel geometry and deterministic metric calculations are stored in PostgreSQL.
- One primary roof geometry visual call per source crop; at most one targeted visual repair call.
- Project evidence resolves roof systems/layers separately from geometry.
- User-confirmed plane/system choices are protected from later automatic assignment updates.
- Projected and true surface areas, edges/upstands, openings, drainage and NRM2 routes are stored separately.
- Concrete roof derived quantities are explicitly marked as derived/reviewable.
