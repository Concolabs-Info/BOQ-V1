This policy describes what Quanto collects when you use the product, and how it's used. It should be read alongside our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. What we collect

- **Account information**: your name, email address, and authentication details, handled by our sign-in provider.
- **Company information**: the company name, registration details, and country you provide when setting up or joining a company.
- **Project content**: drawings, schedules, specifications, quantities, rates, and other files you upload to a project.
- **Usage data**: basic technical information (such as device and browser details) needed to operate and secure the product.

## 2. How we use it

- To create and run your account, company, and projects.
- To generate takeoff and BOQ outputs from the project content you upload, including sending relevant material to third-party AI model providers as described in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
- To communicate with you about your account, such as invitations and security notices.
- To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the product.

We do not sell your project data or personal information.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **AI model providers**, to generate takeoff and BOQ outputs — see the AI section of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for what's sent and why.
- **Infrastructure providers** (hosting, storage, email delivery) who process data on our behalf to run the product.
- We do not share your project content with other companies on Quanto.

## 4. Data retention

We keep your account, company, and project data for as long as your company's account is active. If a company is deleted, project data associated with it is removed; members retain their own account but lose access to that company's projects.

## 5. Your choices

You can update your account details from Settings at any time. To request deletion of your account or company data outside of the normal in-product flows, contact us using the details below.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy: [legal@quanto.app](mailto:legal@quanto.app).
