## 3. How we use AI models (including GPT)

Quanto uses automated analysis to help produce takeoff geometry, classifications, quantities, and BOQ outputs. To do that, we send relevant project material to third-party AI model providers. That material can include:

- PDF drawings and images cropped from those drawings
- Text and tables extracted from drawings, schedules, and specifications
- Quantities, element types, room names, and other structured project data you or the product create
- Instructions and prompts we write so the models return usable takeoff and BOQ results

Providers may include **OpenAI GPT models** and **Google Gemini**, and may change as we operate the product. Those providers process the submitted material to **return outputs** (for example detected geometry, labels, suggested quantities, or draft bill items). That processing is required for Quanto to generate those outputs.

What this means in practice:

- Uploaded drawings and related project data are not only stored in Quanto. Selected parts are transmitted to those model providers so the models can analyse them and send results back.
- Model outputs are a **starting point**. They are not professional advice, a substitute for a qualified quantity surveyor, or a guaranteed measurement. You must review, edit, and confirm results before you rely on them for pricing, tender, or construction.
- You should assume that anything you upload may be sent to a model provider for processing. Do not upload personal data, client information, or drawings you are not permitted to share that way.
- We use the data to run Quanto for your company (including generating, repairing, and displaying outputs). We do not sell your project files as a dataset.

If a provider's own terms apply to their processing, those terms also apply to the material we send them on your behalf.
